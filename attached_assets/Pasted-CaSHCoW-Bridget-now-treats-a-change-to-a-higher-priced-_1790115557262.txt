BRIDGET CONTRACT (live, deployed 7e536d8)
POST /cashcow/membership/change to a higher-priced tier: charges the new
tier's full price at once, never prorated, no refund of the current
period. Returns the membership fields (membership_id, user_id, tier,
price_crowns, joined_at, current_period_start, next_renewal_at,
renewal_day, status, cancel_at_period_end, pending_tier) plus charged
(true), ref_id, express_blessing_allowance_total and
express_blessing_allowance_remaining. Blessings already used in the
replaced period carry across, so the remaining count is the new total
less those used, never below 0. An upgrade clears a pending downgrade.
Errors: 400 invalid body / "user_id is required." / "Unknown membership
tier. Choose believer, disciple, or apostle."; 401; 402 "Insufficient
Crowns. Balance: N, required: P."; 403 guest; 404 "User not found." /
"Membership not found."; 409 "Only an active membership can change
tier." / "Cannot change tier while cancellation is pending."; 503.
A downgrade sets pending_tier, charges nothing, and takes effect at the
renewal, as today.
